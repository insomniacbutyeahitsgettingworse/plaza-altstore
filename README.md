plaza public testing downloads for ios and android.

## ios

1. download the ipa below.
2. open it with altstore.
3. let altstore sign it with your apple id.
4. open plaza and make your account.

[download the ios ipa](https://github.com/insomniacbutyeahitsgettingworse/plaza-altstore/releases/download/v1.0.0-build3/plaza-altstore.ipa)

## android

1. download the apk below.
2. open it on your phone and install it.
3. open plaza and make your account.

[download the android apk](https://github.com/insomniacbutyeahitsgettingworse/plaza-altstore/releases/latest)

new accounts need approval before they can get in.
